Provider Trust

How we calculate trust

Provider Trust combines reported outcomes, completed swap reports, delayed payout reports, reported KYC requests, refund issues and support quality. Unverified reports are labeled and can be moderated.

KYC probability

A signal with limits

The KYC signal is based on provider policy, route, asset pair, amount range and public reports. It helps users understand risk before sending funds, but it cannot predict every provider review.

F.A.Q.

What does Liberty do?

Liberty provides software that lets you choose between exchanges and trade directly with them. We never have access to, receive or transfer your funds.

What fees are included in the rates shown?

All fees are already included in the shown rate: network fees and exchange fees. You do not pay anything extra for using Liberty instead of the exchange directly. We receive a commission for referring the exchange. It comes out of the exchange's fee, so it does not change your rate. If a route ever adds a fee to your amount, it is shown before you confirm.

Scoring

Metodologia Liberty Score

Scorul combina pret net, privacy, viteza, rezilienta si risc. Greutatile se schimba dupa obiectivul ales, iar furnizorii pot fi auditati separat in Provider ledger.

Formula

Semnale masurabile

  • Net compara suma primita fata de cea mai buna ruta disponibila.
  • Privacy puncteaza policy-ul providerului, Tor, frictiunea de cont si semnalele non-custody.
  • Fast favorizeaza rutele cu timp estimat mai mic.
  • Resilient combina uptime, support readiness si calitatea suportului.
  • Risk penalizeaza providerii experimentali sau cu semnale operationale slabe.

Live layer

Ce este real acum

Preturile de piata pot veni live din CoinGecko. Quote-urile de swap intra in clasament doar cand vin din endpoint public sau din backend-ul /api/quotes.

Asta pastreaza MVP-ul onest: arata direct ce e live, ce vine din API si ce trebuie validat inainte de lansare publica.

Parity

Ce luam bun din modelul Trocador

  • Quotes rate floating si fixed, comparate intre furnizori.
  • Trust support readiness, status de tranzactie, limite si suport.
  • Merchant request-uri de plata si fluxuri pentru incasari.
  • Spend strat de carduri/gift/prepaid peste swap.

Liberty layer

Ce adaugam noi

  • Purpose rutare dupa scop: net, privacy, fast, resilient, merchant.
  • Audit log policy, network support, rating si drift estimat.
  • Fallback scara de rute alternative inainte sa trimiti fonduri.
  • Capsule export JSON cu decizia, sursa preturilor si checklist.

Market gap

Problema exacta

Utilizatorul nu vrea un exchange nou. Vrea sa ajunga rapid de la moneda A la moneda B, fara cont, fara custodie si fara sa ghiceasca daca ruta e sigura. Trocador rezolva gasirea ofertelor. Liberty Swap rezolva si decizia: care ruta e potrivita pentru scopul tau si ce trebuie sa pastrezi ca dovada.

Plus value

Unde putem fi mai buni

  • Decision recomandare dupa scop, nu doar dupa pret.
  • Proof Support Capsule pentru suport, contabilitate si audit personal.
  • Fallback rute alternative pregatite inainte de depunere.
  • Trust scor public cu log policy, drift, support readiness, limite si retele.
  • Simple patru intrari: Swap, Pay, Spend, Track.
